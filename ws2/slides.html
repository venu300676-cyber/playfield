Five rules and three tests for every agent

The rules in the scaffold

  • Use only the information in the inputs.
  • Name the source for every item.
  • List gaps and conflicts under NEEDS PM CHECK.
  • Prepare the PM’s decision; never make it.
  • Leave out what must never appear.

The tests

TestFeed itPass when
T1 Does the jobThe full case fileUsable after a short PM review
T2 Time testA stopwatch on the PM’s reviewClearly below the baseline
T3 Trust checkThe case behind your failure modeFlagged for the PM

Paste only the fictional sample data, or data already approved for the tool. The time test shows the hours given back.

The scaffold with these rules is in the kit (Build the agent) and on the printed handout. Rooms fill in the brackets and may tighten the rules, but should not drop them. A failed test followed by a fix makes a better debrief card than three easy passes.

No tool? Be the agent.

8 to 10 minutes. Counts as Level 2. No login, no network.

  1. Pick a “human agent” who did not write the instructions.
  2. Give them only the instructions and the printed sample data.
  3. They follow the instructions literally. Where the instructions are silent, they say “my instructions don’t tell me”.
  4. Everyone notes each gap. Each gap becomes a new rule.
  5. Run the trust check. Does the agent flag what it cannot resolve?
Use this when the tool login fails, the network drops, or the room has no access. It also works well with AI beginners: they see immediately why precise instructions matter. Experts cannot rush past it either.
