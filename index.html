Questions

Frequently asked

Do you teach AI (Applications & Interpretation) too?

Right now everything here is built specifically for Analysis & Approaches (AA) SL and HL, so the group classes and course stay tightly focused on that syllabus.

How do group cohorts compare to 1:1?

Cohorts are for steady, ongoing progress through the syllabus at a much lower cost per hour. 1:1 is best for a specific, urgent gap — an upcoming IA deadline, a resit, or the final stretch before exams.

Can I switch between 1:1, group, and the course?

Yes. Many students start with the self-paced course, join a cohort once they want live accountability, and add 1:1 sessions in the final weeks before exams.

What if a cohort is full?

Cohorts are capped at 6–8 students so everyone gets feedback. Join the waitlist on the Group Classes page and you'll be first in line for the next one.
