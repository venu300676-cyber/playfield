Small-group cohorts

Learn AA SL/HL alongside students at your level — for a fraction of 1:1 cost.

Cohorts are capped at 6–8 students, grouped by syllabus level (SL/HL) and target grade band, and follow a structured week-by-week roadmap through the full syllabus — with the same past-paper focus as 1:1 sessions.

How it works

Same rigour as 1:1. Built for a group.

1

Placement

A short call or diagnostic quiz places you in the right cohort by level and target grade — not just age or school year.

2

Weekly live class

90 minutes, one topic block at a time, working through theory then exam-style questions together.

3

Problem set + feedback

A weekly set of past-paper questions, submitted and returned with written, mark-scheme-based feedback.

4

Ongoing support

A private group chat for daily questions between sessions, plus recordings of every class.
